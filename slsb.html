CASE STUDY / DATA & ANALYTICS

Seneca Lake SEO Project

Sep 2024–Dec 2024

This page details my contributions to the Seneca Lake Scenic Byway’s SEO and content optimization work, including traffic insights, keyword strategy, and early performance gains.

SENECA LAKE SCENIC BYWAY / SEO & CONTENT

01 / CONTEXT

Project Context & Goals

The strategic partnership focused on improving the Seneca Lake Scenic Byway’s web presence, increasing discoverability, and aligning content with high-interest seasonal queries.

I collaborated with US-based partners at a nonprofit organization and presented project findings and recommendations in English to its stakeholders.

02 / DISCOVERY

Analytics-Driven Discovery

We used Google Analytics 4 and Search Console to evaluate site performance. Issues included:

  • High bounce rate on key landing pages
  • Low CTR from search results
  • Missed opportunities in high-volume keyword targeting

03 / EXECUTION

Optimization & Execution

  • CTR improved by 19.67% within one month
  • The number of keywords ranking in the top 10 increased from 477 to 489
  • Clicks on seasonal pages, such as Snowmobiling and Ice Skating, grew by more than 200%
  • I updated meta descriptions, content layout, and page titles in Squarespace

04 / IMPACT

Early Impact & Takeaways

Although the tracking period was short, early results showed positive momentum: CTR, keyword rankings, and user engagement improved. Continued tracking and refinement were recommended to the client.
