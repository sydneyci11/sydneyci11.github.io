02 / ANALYTICS · SEO

Seneca Lake Scenic Byway Google Analytics Project

Sep 2024–Dec 2024

Learn More

What helps visitors discover and engage with a tourism website?

APPROACH & FINDINGS

This consulting project supported a regional tourism group. I analyzed Google Analytics and Google Search Console data, identified issues such as a high bounce rate and low CTR, and implemented an SEO strategy targeting high-volume queries such as “Seneca Lake wineries.”
→ CTR improved by 19.67%. The number of keywords ranking in the top 10 increased from 477 to 489, and traffic on key seasonal pages grew by more than 200%. I edited meta descriptions, restructured content, and optimized page titles in Squarespace.

  • Google Analytics
  • Search Console

05 / BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE

AHDC Diagnostic Price Sensitivity Analysis

Oct 2024–Dec 2024

View AHDC Portfolio View Tableau Visualization

How does diagnostic demand respond to price changes?

APPROACH & FINDINGS

I collaborated with Cornell’s Animal Health Diagnostic Center to analyze test-demand data from 2019 to 2023. The analysis found that a 12% price increase in 2023 was followed by a sharp 58% drop in usage—nearly triple the impact of a similar increase in 2019. I built dynamic Tableau dashboards to visualize these trends and inform strategic pricing decisions.

  • Demand analysis
  • Tableau
