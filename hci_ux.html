INTERNATIONAL STUDENT HUB / KING’S COLLEGE LONDON

01 / CONTEXT

Project Overview

This research explores how digital tools can improve the experience of international students moving to London by creating a central information hub

  • Accommodation support
  • Financial tips and student discounts
  • Language and university adaptation guidance
  • Social networking and student community engagement
  • Mental health and wellbeing support

This project was completed within a ten week HCI module Our goal was to identify real pain points and co design a simple and inclusive solution that helps students adapt connect and thrive

02 / RESEARCH INTENT

Project Goals

Newly arrived students face fragmented info across housing finance transport wellbeing and social life We aimed to co design a central hub that reduces effort improves clarity and supports faster adaptation

  • Understand student pain points through interviews and analysis
  • Develop a user centric solution using design thinking empathize define ideate prototype test
  • Prototype a supportive application that unifies housing finance mental health and social features

03 / RESEARCH & SYNTHESIS

Empathize and Define

  • Thirty plus interviews with international students across campuses
  • Empathy maps personas and problem statements covering finance housing wellbeing social language
  • Journey mapping to identify high stress pain points

04 / IDEATION

Ideation and Brainstorming

  • Workshops using six thinking hats HMW and how now wow matrix
  • Storyboards and sketches to visualise scenarios and narrow scope

05 / DESIGN & VALIDATION

Prototyping and Testing

  • Figma modular flows for accommodation finance mental health and social features
  • Navigation and content hierarchy focused on clarity
  • Usability testing and heuristic review with iteration
  • My focus mental health interface with resource access and peer connection

06 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Role and Contribution

  • Led UX research for the mental health module five in depth interviews synthesis into personas
  • Facilitated ideation workshops and aligned team on scope
  • Owned UI design of the wellbeing flow in Figma
