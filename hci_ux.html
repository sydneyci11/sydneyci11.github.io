INTERNATIONAL STUDENT HUB / KING’S COLLEGE LONDON

01 / CONTEXT

Project Overview

This project explored how a central digital hub could help international students adapt to life in London.

  • Accommodation support
  • Financial tips and student discounts
  • Language and university adaptation guidance
  • Social networking and student community engagement
  • Mental health and well-being support

Completed as part of a ten-week HCI module, the project aimed to identify student pain points and co-design a simple, inclusive solution to help students adapt, connect, and thrive.

02 / RESEARCH INTENT

Project Goals

Newly arrived students often face fragmented information across housing, finances, transportation, well-being, and social life. The project aimed to address these challenges through a central hub designed to reduce effort, improve clarity, and support adaptation.

  • Understand student pain points through interviews and analysis
  • Develop a user-centered solution using the design-thinking process: empathize, define, ideate, prototype, and test
  • Prototype a supportive application that brings together housing, financial, mental health, and social resources

03 / RESEARCH & SYNTHESIS

Empathize and Define

  • The six-person team conducted 30 interviews with international students across campuses
  • Empathy maps, personas, and problem statements were developed around finances, housing, well-being, social connection, and language
  • Journey mapping was used to identify high-stress points

04 / IDEATION

Ideation and Brainstorming

  • Six Thinking Hats, How Might We (HMW) prompts, and the How-Now-Wow matrix were used in ideation workshops
  • Storyboards and sketches were created to visualize scenarios and narrow the project scope

05 / DESIGN & VALIDATION

Prototyping and Testing

  • Modular Figma flows were created for accommodation, finances, mental health, and social features
  • Navigation and content hierarchy were organized for clarity
  • Usability testing and heuristic reviews informed design iterations
  • The mental health interface focused on resource access and peer connection

06 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Role and Contribution

  • I led UX research for the mental health module, conducted five in-depth interviews, and synthesized the findings into personas
  • I facilitated ideation workshops and helped the team align on scope
  • I designed the UI for the well-being flow in Figma
