01 / HCI · USER RESEARCH

HCI Project: Supporting International Students in London

Sep 2023–Dec 2023

View Case Study
THE PROBLEM
New international students face fragmented information and barriers to finding well-being support.
MY CONTRIBUTION

I led the research and design of the mental health support module. From interviews to empathy mapping, from problem definition to Figma prototyping, this project reflects my full-cycle UX process.

THE OUTCOME
An interactive prototype connecting mental health support with the wider student experience.
