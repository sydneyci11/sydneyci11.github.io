CASE STUDY / UX RESEARCH & DESIGN

Seneca Lake UX Redesign Project

Sep 2024–Dec 2024

This page highlights the UX research and design work I contributed to improve digital engagement for the Seneca Lake Scenic Byway.

SENECA LAKE SCENIC BYWAY / UX REDESIGN

01 / CONTEXT

Project Context & Goals

The Byway’s website was underutilized despite strong tourism appeal. Our goal was to improve usability, accessibility, and user engagement by redesigning the digital experience.

I collaborated with US-based partners at the nonprofit organization and presented project findings and recommendations in English to the client and project stakeholders.

02 / RESEARCH

Research & Discovery

We combined qualitative and quantitative methods to understand user needs:

  • Conducted user interviews and journey mapping to uncover pain points in navigation and content flow
  • Analyzed behavioral data (via Google Analytics) to identify bounce-rate issues and low engagement areas
  • Synthesized findings into personas and user journeys to align stakeholders on key challenges

03 / DESIGN

Design & Prototyping

  • Created Figma wireframes and interactive prototypes to visualize improved site structure
  • Tested design iterations with sample users, refining content hierarchy and accessibility
  • Implemented selected design changes via Squarespace to validate improvements

04 / IMPACT

Outcomes & Impact

The redesign produced measurable improvements:

  • Bounce rate reduced by 2%
  • CTR increased by 19.67%
  • Traffic growth of 200%+ on seasonal tourism pages

These results validated the UX approach and provided stakeholders with a roadmap for long-term design system improvements.
