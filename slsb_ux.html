CASE STUDY / UX RESEARCH & DESIGN

Seneca Lake UX Redesign Project

Sep 2024–Dec 2024

This page highlights the UX research and design work I contributed to improving digital engagement for the Seneca Lake Scenic Byway.

SENECA LAKE SCENIC BYWAY / UX REDESIGN

01 / CONTEXT

Project Context & Goals

The Byway’s website had strong tourism appeal but was underused. The redesign aimed to improve usability, accessibility, and user engagement.

I collaborated with US-based partners at a nonprofit organization and presented project findings and recommendations in English to its stakeholders.

02 / RESEARCH

Research & Discovery

Qualitative and quantitative methods were combined to understand user needs:

  • User interviews and journey mapping were used to identify navigation and content-flow issues
  • Behavioral data from Google Analytics were analyzed to identify bounce-rate issues and areas with low engagement
  • Findings were synthesized into personas and user journeys to align stakeholders on key challenges

03 / DESIGN

Design & Prototyping

  • Figma wireframes and interactive prototypes were created to test an improved site structure
  • Design iterations were tested with sample users, and the content hierarchy and accessibility were refined
  • Selected design changes were implemented in Squarespace for evaluation

04 / IMPACT

Outcomes & Impact

The redesign produced measurable improvements:

  • A 2-percentage-point decrease in bounce rate was observed compared with the previous measurement
  • CTR increased by 19.67%
  • Traffic on seasonal tourism pages grew by more than 200%

These results supported the UX approach and informed a roadmap for long-term design improvements.
